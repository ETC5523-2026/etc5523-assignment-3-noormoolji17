## The blog post

The post adapts my Assignment 2 report on Zacher et al. (2019), which estimated the burden of
healthcare-associated infections in Germany, for a general audience. It explains why Germany has
a lower infection rate among hospital patients than the EU/EEA average, yet loses more healthy
life across its whole population.
